Processing, the same for every file (ffmpeg):

- cropped to 16:10 around the car and scaled to 1600x1000 with Lanczos (hero.jpg: 2:1, 2400x1200);
- licence plates and photographer stickers replaced by a soft smudge (area-downscaled, blurred patch);
- light grade: contrast 1.05, saturation 0.94, gamma 0.98; JPEG quality 4.
