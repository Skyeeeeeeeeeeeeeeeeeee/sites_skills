## Scope

Whole site for «Каретный»: index.html (home), fleet.html, car.html, booking.html, terms.html. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, fleet, car), Operate (booking form).

## Audience and action

Affluent Moscow clients and their assistants booking a premium car with or without a chauffeur. Primary action: a booking request (car, date, time, address, driver option, contact). One CTA label site-wide: «Забронировать».

## Direction contract

THESIS: A rental is a carriage call. The site is a doorman's call slip, «талон подачи», that the visitor fills in. It refuses the category default of a dark hero over a glossy supercar with a gold accent and a search bar.

OWN-WORLD: Light warm-monochrome paper (#FBFBFA), charcoal ink, 1px #EAEAEA rules, a single cobalt stamp ink accent used for the stamp, active states and the primary CTA focus. Old Standard TT (19th-century Russian book type) for display, Onest for UI, JetBrains Mono for slip data (times, prices, plate-like codes). 8px cards, 6px controls, a perforated slip edge.

STORY: The visitor sees the house and its garage on Karetny Ryad, picks a car, sees the exact price and what happens next, then submits the slip, which gets stamped «ПРИНЯТО».

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: wordmark nav; a two-line display headline; one line of subtext; «Забронировать» and «Автопарк». Right 5/12: a large car photograph with the live call slip overlapping its lower-left corner (car chips, driver toggle, date, time, live price). Primary action is on the slip itself.

FORM: Valet call slip, grounded candidate 3 of 7, seed key 023fb55a (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

The live call slip: every choice fills a line on the slip in mono, the total recalculates, and on submit a cobalt «ПРИНЯТО» stamp lands with a short press animation.
